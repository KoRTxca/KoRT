## 1. Mission & Vision
The Knights of the Round Table (KoRT) is a peer-powered ecosystem and parallel infrastructure designed to patch a broken, isolating societal system. It leverages AI, mesh networks, and a localized digital economy to create a decentralized network of human-centered round tables.

Project Mayhem is the operational deployment phase: mobilizing the Digital Dollars (RTD$) economy, initiating hardware recycling ("Compukids" integration), and activating the local mesh network in Cowichan Valley.
