The Origin

20 Years of Doing This Without a Name.

"People in this community have been calling each other instead of 911 for decades. We remove the partner who won't leave. We sit with someone in crisis at 3am. We show up when the system doesn't. Now we're building the infrastructure to do this properly — licensed, insured, funded, permanent."

KoRT is built on lived experience. This isn't a startup idea — it's the formalization of something that already works. Based on the CAHOOTS model (Eugene, OR — 1989), but with one critical difference: KoRT depends on no government funding. Ever.

🕊Dedicated to Aaron Van Nice and Kyle "Krymes" Dunn — who showed us what this looks like done with love.
