### Full Description (4000 chars max)

**KoRT is what happens when your community decides nobody gets left behind.**

Knights of the Round Table (KoRT) is a peer-powered community platform built around one idea: when people are in crisis, they deserve a human response — not a police response. KoRT is the "4th option for 911."

**WHAT YOU GET WITH KoRT:**

🛡️ **The Watch — Crisis Support**
Request peer support with one tap. Trained community peers respond to mental health calls, substance concerns, housing crises, and welfare checks. Not police. Not ambulances. Neighbours.

💰 **The Treasury — Earn Real Money**
Zero-cost earning path: follow our guided Quick Stack and earn $15–30 in your first 48 hours. Affiliate programs, referral chains, and community earning — shared with the whole Round Table.

🌿 **The Healer's Keep — Recovery Support**
Street-smart harm reduction. No shame. No clinical distance. AI Sponsor available 24/7. Harm reduction dictionary, distraction engine, free courses, one-tap SOS.

📜 **The Herald — Navigate the System**
ICBC claims, PWD applications, housing disputes, legal letters. We're not lawyers — we're peers who know the terrain.

⚔️ **The Armory — Know Your Rights**
BC legal rights, plain language. What to do when police approach. Tenant rights. Employment rights. Your rights as a patient.

🎓 **The Academy — Earn Your Rank**
Training pathway from Page to Knight. Peer support certification prep, crisis response protocols, de-escalation training.

👥 **The Court — Your Community**
Searchable member directory. Skills exchange. Gig marketplace. Barter. Joint ventures. The community you've been looking for.

**THE ROUND TABLE ECONOMY**
Every member earns Round Table Dollars (RTD) through participation. 60% of affiliate earnings go to you. 40% goes to the Community Pool — redistributed to all active members. You earn when others earn. That's the Round Table.

**BUILT IN BC, FOR BC**
Founded in Ladysmith, BC. Focused on Vancouver Island and Cowichan Valley communities that have been underserved for too long.

*"No one gets left behind."*

**IMPORTANT:** KoRT is peer support and mutual aid, not a licensed emergency service. For life-threatening emergencies, always call 911.

## FLUTTER TECH STACK NOTES (for developer submission)

- Framework: Flutter (Dart) — cross-platform iOS + Android
- Minimum iOS: 14.0
- Minimum Android: API 24 (Android 7.0)
- Backend: Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Realtime)
- Push notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging
- Maps: Google Maps SDK (for responder routing)
- Auth: Supabase SSO (email + magic link)
- Payments: Stripe (CAD) — in-app subscription management
- Privacy: No data sold. Location only used when SOS/responder active.
