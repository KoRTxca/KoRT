## 4. The Round Table Dollar (RTD)

RTD is KoRT's internal community ledger unit. It is:
- **Not a security or investment**
- **Not cryptocurrency**
- **Not redeemable for cash directly** (redeemable for goods/services through KoRT's partner ecosystem)
- A community accounting tool that tracks contribution, earning, and equity participation

RTD balances are maintained at KoRT's discretion. KoRT reserves the right to adjust the RTD system with 30 days notice to members.

## 5. The Crisis Response Program (The Watch)

KoRT's crisis response program operates as **peer support and mutual aid only**. This is critical:

- KoRT responders are trained community peers, not licensed medical professionals, police, or emergency services
- KoRT cannot and does not provide emergency medical services
- KoRT cannot involuntarily apprehend any individual (this authority belongs to police and physicians under the BC Mental Health Act)
- If you are in a life-threatening emergency, **call 911 immediately**
- KoRT's involvement in any crisis is entirely voluntary for all parties

**By using The Watch (crisis response feature), you acknowledge:**
- KoRT is providing peer support and mutual aid, not emergency services
- KoRT responders act in good faith as community volunteers
- KoRT, Xception Contracting Ltd., and individual responders are not liable for outcomes of peer support interactions
- You consent to your general location being shared with nearby KoRT responders when you activate SOS
